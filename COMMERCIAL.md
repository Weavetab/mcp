## 1. When the Open Source License Applies (Free)

You may freely use, modify, and distribute Weavetab under the AGPLv3 open-source license if:
- **Your product is open source**: Any software, agent pipeline, SaaS, or service that incorporates, modifies, or exposes Weavetab over a network is distributed with its complete source code under AGPLv3.
- **Personal and non-commercial development**: Personal automation scripts, academic research, and community plugin authoring.
- **Public plugins**: Developing and publishing open-source plugins using `@weavetab/sdk` to npm or the Weavetab registry.

---

## 2. When a Commercial License Is Required

A **Weavetab Commercial License** is required if:
1. **Proprietary SaaS & Cloud Integration**: You embed, host, or execute Weavetab within a closed-source, proprietary commercial platform, API, or customer-facing product without disclosing your source code under AGPLv3.
2. **Private Internal Enterprise Use**: Your organization's corporate policies or legal compliance frameworks prohibit the use of copyleft (GPL / AGPL) licenses in internal tooling or agent infrastructure.
3. **Distribution in Closed-Source Software**: You distribute Weavetab binaries, drivers, or engines bundled within proprietary Electron or desktop applications.
4. **Custom SLAs & Indemnification**: You require dedicated enterprise support, guaranteed uptime SLAs, customized feature prioritization, or intellectual property warranty and indemnification.
